CONTEXT, NOT VIBES

Three ways a game tightens.

Pressure is assigned before each delivery from the live innings state.

01
FINISH PHASE ANY INNINGS

The last five overs.

30 or fewer legal deliveries remain. Every score or stop has a direct effect on the finish.

02
WICKET SQUEEZE ANY INNINGS

Five down, time short.

At least five wickets have fallen, after over eight, with no more than 12 overs left.

03
CHASE SQUEEZE SECOND INNINGS

The asking rate bites.

After three overs: required rate is 9.5+, or 7.5+ and at least 2.5 above the current rate.

OPEN METHOD

A score you can
take apart.

No black box. The rating combines pressure output, change from the player's own usual level, and the share of pressure matches that beat that baseline.

Open report ↗
01

Compare like with like

Metrics are standardized within each format-season pool, each player against the other eligible players in their own pool (three other pool rules were tested for the small T20I pools and not adopted), then blended by pressure-ball volume. This avoids treating era or format scoring conditions as player skill.

02

Reward more than a hot spell

Default eligibility requires 60 pressure balls, eight pressure matches, and 60 usual balls. Smaller samples can qualify only when the filter is deliberately relaxed.

03

Shrink uncertain scores

The index is pulled toward 50 when pressure-ball or match volume is low. Confidence is displayed beside every selection.

04

Check consistency across matches

The ranking includes the percentage of pressure matches with positive performance relative to the player's usual baseline, not just an aggregate strike or economy rate.

05

Score all-rounders across both disciplines

All-rounders must qualify for both the batting and bowling lists. Their composite index is the equal-weight mean of those two reliability-adjusted indexes; both component scores remain visible.

06

Exclude retired IPL players

Six confirmed IPL playing retirements (Pollard, Bravo, Rayudu, Karthik, Ashwin, Mohit Sharma) are excluded from player rankings, as are players who appear for Pakistan in the supplied T20I data (that last rule matters only when All or T20I is selected; none of those players has an IPL record). Historical source rows remain intact. The report covers the IPL only and shows the top 10 with no exclusions, with full metrics, as a labelled alternative. One line in scoring.mjs (APPLY_PLAYER_EXCLUSIONS) switches the exclusions off.

07

Test how stable the lists are

The IPL report adds charts and sensitivity tests: the reading of the undocumented extras type (tested on IPL matches, and it kept the submitted method), the effect of the exclusions and of the sample thresholds, and the names that stay in the top 10 under every setting. It also re-samples the data to show how often each place holds, and it explains why a player with a high usual level can rank below one who improves more: the index measures improvement over a player's own usual level, not absolute pressure quality. The extras decision rule was fixed in the plan after a scouting run and was not committed beforehand.
