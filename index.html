CONTEXT, NOT VIBES

Three ways a game tightens.

Pressure is assigned before each delivery from the live innings state.

01
FINISH PHASE ANY INNINGS

The last five overs.

30 or fewer legal deliveries remain. Every score or stop has a direct effect on the finish.

02
WICKET SQUEEZE ANY INNINGS

Five down, time short.

At least five wickets have fallen, after over eight, with no more than 12 overs left.

03
CHASE SQUEEZE SECOND INNINGS

The asking rate bites.

After three overs: required rate is 9.5+, or 7.5+ and at least 2.5 above the current rate.

OPEN METHOD

A score you can
take apart.

No black box. The rating combines pressure output, change from the player's own usual level, and the share of pressure matches that beat that baseline.

Open report ↗
01

Compare like with like

Metrics are standardized within each format-season pool, then blended by pressure-ball volume. This avoids treating era or format scoring conditions as player skill.

02

Reward more than a hot spell

Default eligibility requires 60 pressure balls, eight pressure matches, and 60 usual balls. Smaller samples can qualify only when the filter is deliberately relaxed.

03

Shrink uncertain scores

The index is pulled toward 50 when pressure-ball or match volume is low. Confidence is displayed beside every selection.

04

Check consistency across matches

The ranking includes the percentage of pressure matches with positive performance relative to the player's usual baseline, not just an aggregate strike or economy rate.

05

Score all-rounders across both disciplines

All-rounders must qualify for both the batting and bowling lists. Their composite index is the equal-weight mean of those two reliability-adjusted indexes; both component scores remain visible.

06

Exclude unavailable IPL 2027 players

Confirmed IPL retirements and reported 2027 coaching appointments, including Mohit Sharma's expected CSK bowling-coach move, are excluded from player rankings. Historical source rows remain intact.
